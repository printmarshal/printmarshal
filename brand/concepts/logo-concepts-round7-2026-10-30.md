# App icon concepts, round 7 — 30 October 2026 (draft, for choosing)

Brief: round-6 D (the layered MFP with a translucent second machine behind) is the favourite. Replace
its square corner tile with a shield carrying the P, and show the same icon with its colours reversed.
Sketches on `logo-sketches-round7-2026-10-30.svg` (rendered as `.png`), each at 240 px, 96 px, 48 px
and 24 px.

- **D1. Layered MFP, shield with P.** Cobalt icon; ghost machine behind, shaded white machine in
  front, a Deep shield at the bottom-left corner with a white Barlow P. A later app keeps the shield
  and changes the letter. At 24 px it reduces to the shield alone.
- **D2. The same, reversed.** Mist icon; the machine in Cobalt with the light showing through the
  gaps, a paler ghost behind, and a white shield with the P in Cobalt. Same palette, swapped.

## Notes
The shield is the round-3 small-shield path, so it matches the badge used on the earlier marks. The
P is Barlow Bold, fetched for rendering; production files should outline the text. Shadows are SVG
drop-shadow filters and will need baking for raster exports.
