# App icon concepts, round 4 — 30 October 2026 (draft, for choosing)

Brief: variants of round-3 concept C (the shield holding the MFP) and E (the Graphite icon), with
no brass anywhere, no status dot, and a simpler drawing. Sketches on
`logo-sketches-round4-2026-10-30.svg` (rendered as `.png`), each at 160 px, 96 px, 32 px and 16 px.

1. **C, cleaned.** The round-3 shield outline with the dot removed: white line work, full MFP
   (feeder, lid, panel, slot, two drawers, casters) on the Cobalt icon. The baseline.
2. **C on Graphite.** The same mark in Mist on E's Graphite icon. Matches the console and the dark
   site sections; quieter in a dock.
3. **Wider shield, simpler MFP.** A squarer shield gives the machine more room; the MFP loses the
   feeder, handles and casters and grows. Fewer lines, bigger read.
4. **Solid shield, Cobalt.** The shield is a solid white plate and the MFP is drawn into it in Cobalt.
   Strongest at a glance and the best 16 px of the set, because the shield is a shape, not a line.
5. **Solid shield, Graphite.** The same plate on Graphite: Mist shield, Graphite MFP. Two palette
   colours and no blue, the most neutral of the six.
6. **Light icon and glyph.** Graphite line work on a Mist icon for light contexts, plus the one-ink
   glyph (Graphite or Cobalt) for favicon, tab bar and console sidebar.

## Where I would start
4 is the strongest icon: it reads as a shield from across the room and as a printer up close, and it
is the only one that still says "shield with a machine in it" at 16 px. 5 is its Graphite twin for
the dark console. If the outline feel of C is the point, 3 is the better outline than 1 because the
simpler MFP is what makes the shield-plus-printer read at small sizes.
