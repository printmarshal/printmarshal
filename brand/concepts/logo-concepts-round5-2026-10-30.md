# App icon concepts, round 5 — 30 October 2026 (draft, for choosing)

Three groups on `logo-sketches-round5-2026-10-30.svg` (rendered as `.png`), each icon at 160 px,
96 px, 32 px and 16 px.

## Kept from round 4
1. **Shield and MFP, Cobalt.** White outline shield holding the full MFP.
2. **Shield and MFP, Graphite.** The same mark in Mist on Graphite.
6. **Light icon, same mark.** Graphite on Mist, now drawn with the same shield and full MFP as 1 and 2
   (round 4 had used the wider shield and simpler machine), plus the one-ink glyphs.

## The word instead of the picture
- **W1. The word, Cobalt.** Lowercase mono "print" from wordmark A, white on Cobalt, the way Zoom's
  icon is only its name. Falls back to a single "p" from 32 px down.
- **W2. The word with a cursor, Graphite.** "print" in Mist with a Cobalt block cursor after it: a
  terminal prompt, the developer read. The cursor is the only colour.
- **W3. The word in the shield.** The kept shield with "print" inside instead of the MFP, for a direct
  comparison with mark 1.

## Office-style: filled MFP with a letter tile
The MFP is drawn as solid white blocks and the icon background shows through the gaps as the
outline. A rounded tile sits at the bottom-left corner with a letter, the way Word, Excel and Outlook
carry W, X and O.
- **O1. "P" tile** on a Deep tile over the Cobalt icon.
- **O2. "PM" tile**, wider, for Print Marshal specifically.
- **O3. Graphite base with a Cobalt tile**: the suite colour lives in the tile, so each later app keeps
  the Graphite base and changes the tile colour, letter and picture.

## Notes
Fonts in the sketch are IBM Plex Mono Medium and Barlow Bold, fetched for rendering; production
files should outline the text. The word icons stop being readable below 48 px and need the single
letter; the Office-style icons reduce to the tile alone at 16 px, which is also how Office does it.
